Ubiquiti WIFI Extender Setup: Complete Connection Guide

What is Ubiquiti WiFi Extender? A Ubiquiti WiFi Extender is a powerful device designed to improve your wireless network's range and performance.

February 04, 2025

What is Ubiquiti WiFi Extender?

A Ubiquiti WiFi Extender is a powerful device designed to improve your wireless network's range and performance. Unlike standard routers, this extender uses advanced technology to eliminate dead zones in your home or office, ensuring consistent connectivity. It is especially useful in larger spaces where the signal from your existing modem or router struggles to reach certain areas. By acting as a bridge between your router and devices, the Ubiquiti WiFi Extender allows for uninterrupted internet access, even in challenging environments.

 

Benefits of using Ubiquiti WIFI Extender

Using a Ubiquiti WiFi Extender offers several benefits, particularly for users with high internet demands. First, it significantly boosts the number of devices connecting simultaneously, from about 12 to up to 300. This makes it ideal for smart homes or offices where multiple devices are constantly in use. Second, it enhances signal strength and speed, providing a seamless experience for streaming, gaming, or video conferencing. Lastly, it supports dual-band frequencies (2.4GHz and 5GHz), ensuring flexibility and compatibility with various devices. With its central placement under the ceiling, the extender ensures optimal signal distribution throughout your space.

How to connect Ubiquiti WIFI Extender

1. Unbox the device: Remove the device from its packaging and ensure you have all the necessary components, including power adapters and cables. Ubiquiti uses P.O.E (Power Over Ethernet), so you will require the correct P.O.E power supply.

2. Connect to Power and Data: Connect a data cable from the original modem/router to the POE adapter, then run the data cable to the Ubiquiti device. Ideally, place the device centrally in your home, under the ceiling (data cables are usually run through the ceiling space).

3. Prepare for Installation: Before installing, take a clear picture of the barcode on the back of the Ubiquiti device, as you’ll need it later for app configuration.

4. Install the UBIQUITI App: Download and open the Ubiquiti App. Select "Add Device" and scan the barcode when prompted.

5. Configure the Device: Once added, configure the Wi-Fi name and password for both 2.4GHz and 5GHz frequencies.

6. Optimize Device Settings: If multiple devices are connected to your existing router, use the same Wi-Fi name and password for the Ubiquiti extender to maintain continuity.

7. Disable Original Wi-Fi: After setup, switch off the Wi-Fi on your original modem/router to fully utilize the extender's capabilities.
