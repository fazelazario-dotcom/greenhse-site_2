Common Lighting Mistakes Perth Homeowners Make (And How to Avoid Them)

Lighting plays a more prominent role in your home's appearance and everyday living experience than you might expect.

July 21, 2026

Lighting plays a more prominent role in your home's appearance and everyday living experience than you might expect. The right lighting enhances comfort, improves functionality, and highlights your interior and outdoor design. However, many Perth homeowners unknowingly make lighting choices that leave their homes feeling dull, uncomfortable, or inefficient.

Whether you're building a new home, renovating an existing property, or replacing outdated fixtures, avoiding a few common mistakes can help you create a brighter, more inviting living space. 

Let's check out a common mistake when choosing lighting and the points to consider to select the right lighting for your home.

 

1. Choosing the Wrong Colour Temperature

One of the most common mistakes is selecting lights based solely on brightness without considering colour temperature. The colour of your light has a significant impact on the mood and functionality of each room.

  • Warm White (2700K–3000K): Ideal for living rooms, bedrooms, and dining areas where a cozy atmosphere is preferred.
  • Cool White (4000K): Great for kitchens, bathrooms, laundries, and workspaces where clear visibility is important.
  • Daylight (5000K–6500K): Best suited for garages, workshops, or commercial environments that require maximum brightness.

Using cool white lighting throughout the entire home can make living spaces feel harsh, while using warm lighting in task-oriented areas may reduce visibility.

Recommended products: LED Downlights, LED Bulbs, Pendant Lights with selectable colour temperatures.

 

2. Relying on a Single Light Source

Many homes rely on a single central ceiling light to illuminate an entire room. While it may provide general lighting, it can't deliver the proper lighting to each corner of the home as we need.

A well-designed lighting plan uses three layers:

  • Ambient lighting for overall illumination.
  • Task lighting for activities like cooking, reading, or working.
  • Accent lighting to highlight artwork, shelving, or architectural features.

Combining these layers creates a balanced space that is both functional and visually appealing.

Recommended products: Ceiling Lights, Pendant Lights, Wall Lights, LED Strip Lighting, Table Lamps.

 

3. Ignoring Perth's Natural Sunlight

Perth enjoys more sunshine than most Australian cities, making natural light one of your home's greatest assets. Unfortunately, many homeowners fail to consider how artificial lighting should complement daylight.

Instead of competing with natural light:

  • Position lighting to reduce shadows.
  • Install dimmable LED fixtures to adjust brightness throughout the day.
  • Choose lighting that complements rather than overpowers daylight.

This approach improves comfort while reducing unnecessary electricity usage.

Recommended products: Dimmable LED Downlights, Smart Lighting Systems, LED Panels.

 

4. Using the Wrong Brightness

Many people still choose lighting based on watts instead of lumens. Watts measure energy consumption, while lumens measure actual brightness.

As a general guide:

  • Living Room: 1,500–3,000 lumens
  • Kitchen: 3,000–4,000 lumens
  • Bedroom: 1,000–2,000 lumens
  • Bathroom: 2,000–4,000 lumens
  • Home Office: 3,000 lumens or more

Selecting the appropriate brightness ensures every room is comfortable without feeling too dark or overly bright.

 

5. Overlooking Outdoor Lighting

Outdoor lighting isn't just about improving your home's appearance; it also enhances safety, security, and usability after sunset.

Common mistakes include:

  • Poorly lit pathways
  • Dark entryways
  • Inadequate driveway lighting
  • Ignoring patios and entertaining areas
  • Forgetting garden feature lighting

A thoughtful outdoor lighting design makes your property safer while creating an inviting atmosphere for family and guests.

Recommended products: Outdoor Wall Lights, Garden Lights, Bollard Lights, Flood Lights, Security Lights.

 

6. Choosing Style Over Function

It's easy to fall in love with a beautiful light fixture, but appearance should never come at the expense of practicality.

For example:

  • Decorative pendants should provide adequate light over kitchen islands.
  • Bathroom fixtures should offer clear, even illumination around mirrors.
  • Home offices require task lighting that reduces eye strain.

The best lighting combines attractive design with everyday functionality.

 

7. Forgetting About Energy Efficiency

Lighting technology is changing day by day, and LED lighting has become the preferred choice for modern homes in Perth. LED lights are more energy-efficient than traditional lighting.

Benefits of using LED lighting include:

  • Lower electricity consumption
  • Longer lifespan than traditional bulbs
  • Reduced maintenance
  • Better light quality
  • Environmentally friendly performance

Upgrading older halogen or incandescent fixtures to LED can significantly reduce household energy costs over time.

Recommended products: LED Downlights, LED Bulbs, LED Panels, Smart LED Lighting.

 

8. Right Placement of Lighting

Sometimes people make a mistake in placing the right lighting in the right place. Even premium lighting products won't perform well if they're installed in the wrong location.

Common placement mistakes include:

  • Pendant lights hung too high or too low.
  • Uneven spacing between downlights.
  • Shadows on kitchen benchtops.
  • Insufficient lighting near mirrors.
  • Dark corners in living rooms.

Carefully planning fixture placement ensures every area of your home receives the right amount of illumination.

 

9. Not Using Smart Lighting

The lighting technology is becoming more advanced day by day. Smart lighting products are available at the Greenhse lighting store in Perth.

Modern lighting is about more than simply turning lights on and off.

Smart lighting systems allow homeowners to:

  • Adjust brightness with dimmers.
  • Schedule lighting automatically.
  • Control lights remotely using a smartphone.
  • Create customised lighting scenes.
  • Improve energy efficiency through automation.

These features offer greater convenience while helping reduce unnecessary energy usage.

Recommended products: Smart LED Bulbs, Smart Switches, Motion Sensor Lights, Dimmers.

 

10. Buying Cheap, Low-Quality Lighting Fixtures

While budget-friendly fixtures may seem like a good deal initially, they often have shorter lifespans, inconsistent light output, and lower build quality.

Investing in quality lighting provides:

  • Better durability
  • Reliable performance
  • Improved safety
  • Superior energy efficiency
  • Lower replacement costs over time

Choosing well-made fixtures is a long-term investment that benefits both your home and your wallet.

 

When Should You Upgrade Your Home Lighting?

If you've noticed any of the following, it may be time for an upgrade:

  • Flickering or inconsistent lighting
  • High electricity bills
  • Outdated halogen or fluorescent fixtures
  • Rooms that feel too dark
  • Poor visibility while cooking or working
  • Limited lighting control or flexibility

Replacing outdated fixtures with modern LED solutions can instantly improve comfort, aesthetics, and energy efficiency throughout your home.

 

Final Thoughts

Lighting has the power to transform the way your home looks, feels, and functions. By avoiding these common mistakes, you can create spaces that are brighter, more comfortable, and more energy-efficient while adding value to your property.

Whether you're refreshing a single room or planning a complete home renovation, choosing the right lighting products is just as important as selecting the right furniture or paint colours. From LED Downlights and Indoor Lights to Outdoor Lighting, Smart Lighting, and Energy-Efficient LED Solutions, investing in quality products ensures lasting performance and a better living experience.
