Understanding Strip Lighting - A Practical Guide to Choosing the Right Solution

Learn how to choose the right strip lighting product for your space. Explore types, features, and expert tips from Greenhse Technologies' trusted lighting store in Perth.

Strip lighting is one of the most versatile and powerful lighting solutions available today. It allows you to create stunning architectural features while also delivering highly functional, practical lighting.

However, there is no single strip light that does everything. The reason there are so many options (often 40+ types) is simple — each one is designed for a specific purpose, with its own strengths and limitations.

This guide by Greenhse Perth breaks down the key factors to help you confidently choose the right strip lighting for your application. 

1. Length & Run Capability

One of the most important considerations is how far your strip lighting needs to run.

  • 12V Strip Lighting
    • Higher current, shorter run lengths
    • Suitable for small applications
  • 24V Strip Lighting
    • Lower current and reduced heat
    • Typically up to 5 metres per run
  • Long-Run 24V Strip
    • Up to 20 metres from one end
    • Up to 40 metres when powered from both ends
    • No visible voltage drop
  • 240V Strip Lighting
    • Ideal for very long runs (up to 50 metres)
    • Minimal voltage drop and lower heat
    • No driver required

2. Colour Options

Colour selection impacts both aesthetics and functionality.

  • RGB (Full Colour)
    • Millions of colour options
    • White light is created by mixing colours (often less natural)
  • CCT (Tunable White)
    • Adjustable white light (typically 2700K–6500K)
    • Ideal for spaces requiring colour flexibility
  • Single Colour
    • Consistent, high-quality white light
    • Available in common tones:
      • 2700K (Warm)
      • 3000K (Warm White)
      • 4000K (Neutral)
      • 5500K–6000K (Cool/Daylight)

Key points:
 If you want quality white light → choose Single Colour or CCT
 If you want effects → choose RGB

3. Brightness & Performance

Brightness is measured in lumens, not watts.

  • Higher wattage generally = more light
  • Efficiency matters more than power
  • Strip lights with 150 lumens/Watt or higher will not only produce more light, but also lower power bills.

Typical Performance Range

  • Greenhse have strip lights from 7.5W/m with 700 lumens to 23W/m with over 3500 Lumens/meter.

(As a reference a standard 8-10Watt Downlighting has about 900 lumens) 

  • Entry level: ~700 lumens/m
  • High performance: 3500+ lumens/m

Practical Guidelines

  • Display lighting / niches: High brightness required
  • Under-cabinet (2–3m):
    • ~12W/m
    • 1800+ lumens/m ideal

Dimming

  • All strip lights can be dimmed
  • Down to <5% brightness
  • Improves energy efficiency and control

Key Points:
 Focus on lumens and efficiency, not just wattage.

4. Quality Matters

Quality affects appearance, performance, and lifespan.

Key Factors:

  • LED Density
    • Higher density = smoother, dot-free light
  • CRI (Colour Rendering Index)
    • CRI 90+ = accurate, vibrant colours
    • CRI 70 = lower quality output
  • PCB Construction
    • 10mm PCB = stronger and more durable than 5mm
    • 3 OZZ (3 x PCB copper layers)= better heat handling and longevity
  • Efficiency
    • 160 lm/W uses significantly less power than 80 lm/W
  • Voltage
    • 24V = lower heat and longer lifespan vs 12V
  • Coatings
    • Improve durability and weather resistance
    • Chip design and edging can prevent light separation
  • Warranty
    • Premium strips offer up to 5 years

Key takeaway:
 Better quality = better light, longer life, and fewer issues.

5. Controllers & Smart Control

Controllers determine how your strip lighting operates.

  • Single Colour
    • No controller required
    • Optional dimming modules
  • CCT
    • Required for colour temperature control
  • RGB
    • Required for colour selection and effects

Control Options:

  • Handheld remotes
  • Wall switches
  • Smartphone apps
  • Voice control (Google / Alexa)
  • Integration with sensors or automation systems

Key takeaway:
 Controllers unlock flexibility, automation, and smart home integration.

6. Channels & Installation

Aluminium channels are often essential for a clean, professional finish.

Benefits:

  • Improve heat dissipation
  • Extend lifespan
  • Create a premium, architectural look
  • Diffuse light for a smoother effect

Common Types:

  • Recessed (wing channels) – built into cabinetry
  • Surface-mounted – ideal for retrofits

Finishes:

  • Aluminium (standard)
  • Black or white (premium options)

Diffusers:

  • Typically 15–20% diffusion which help create a uniform and diffused light
  • Reduce glare and hide LED dots

Key Points:
 Channels elevate both performance and aesthetics.

Final Thoughts

Choosing the right strip lighting comes down to three key questions that clear your mind.

  • How long is the run?
  • What colour flexibility do you need?
  • How bright does the space need to be?

Once these are clear, selecting the right product becomes simple for you.

If done correctly, strip lighting doesn't just illuminate a space — it transforms it.
