With advancements in lens technology and impressive lumens per watt, Greenhse Technologies Area Lights are designed to illuminate vast spaces effectively, even when mounted at lower heights. These lights offer reliable performance with minimal energy consumption, whether for industrial yards, sports stadiums, or parking lots.

 

What is an Area/Street Light? 

An area light emits light from a defined surface rather than a single point like spotlights. The resulting light creates soft shadows and a more realistic lighting effect, which minmises shadows and creates even lighting. 

Area lights are used where high-powered lighting is required, i.e., sports stadiums, parking lots, tennis courts, etc. The lights have adjustable beam angles to cover different areas of the field.

Area lights are beneficial as they provide high brightness (lumens), lower power consumption, and are extremely energy efficient with long lifespans. They offer adjustable beam angles to cover different areas and have anti-glare optics to prevent blinding players and spectators. 

 

Uses of LED Area Lights

LED Area Lights are versatile and suitable for both outdoor and indoor applications. Some key use cases include:

  • Sports Facilities
  • Parking Lots
  • Industrial Yards and Warehouses
  • Commercial and Hospitality Venues
