Why Choose High Bay LED Lights for Your Industrial Space?

LED Highbay lights can offer savings of up to 80% compared to older metal Halide or other older lighting options.

Greenhouse Highbays ensure high energy savings, as well as optimising the light for different applications and ceiling heights.

Utilising specially designed lenses Greenhouse Highbays are a lot more effective. High Lumens per Watt also increase the efficiency, as the same output can be achieved with less power consumption.

Image showing beam angles and heights.

Efficiency and effectiveness are important when selecting a highway but quality and lifespan are just as important.  Greenhouse highbay lights can last over 50 000hrs even in harsh environments. Several elements are very important to ensuring a Highbay light will last a long time - housing design, driver and LED chips. Greenhouse Highbay lights now utilise Sosen drivers, which after 13 years of testing have proved to be more reliable than any other driver. Since moving to these drivers there has not been a single failure in over 6 years. Good quality LED chips with high LSM 70 values and well-designed aluminium alloy housings allow a good heatsink with effective cooling. 

Long lifespans are not only important for ensuring a good return on investment but critical in eliminating frequent changes that are very expensive due to the height of installation of the high bay. 

Cheaper Highbay options may utilise DOB or driver on board. These provide lower lumens per watt, much shorter lifespans,  have a frequency that could affect working with certain machinery, and are more sensitive to power surges and failure. The prices are however attractive, and these can be suitable for home sheds or areas that are not used as often.
