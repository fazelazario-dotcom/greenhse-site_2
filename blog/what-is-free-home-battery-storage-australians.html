FREE Home Battery Storage for Australians - What Does it Mean for You?

Discover what free home battery storage means for Australians, how it works, and the key benefits it offers to help you save on energy costs and improve home efficiency.

The Australian Federal Government has announced a $2.3 billion investment in home battery systems, supporting Australia’s transition to clean energy and easing demand on the national electricity grid. 

Read more: Federal Government Cheaper Home Battery Program

 

How the Rebate Works

Homeowners can now access rebates of up to $370 per kWh for batteries certified by the Clean Energy Council. This incentive is tied to Small-Scale Technology Certificates (STCs) and will gradually reduce from July 2025 through July 2030.

Read more: More on STCs
