15W/8W, 83mm Gimbal Low Glare Square Downlights

83mm cutoutGimbal 0–360°Triac dimmable
$28.00 ex GST · $30.80 inc GST

A low glare square gimbal downlight for offices and upmarket homes — made to highlight pictures and wall features, and for public areas such as stairways, reception areas and corridors. The head tilts 23° and rotates 0–360°, with the colour temperature switchable between warm white 3000K and natural white 4000K. Available as an 8W single (660–760 lumens) or 15W double (1300–1500 lumens), each on an 83mm cutout with a tight 36° low-glare beam. Triac dimmable, in matte white or matte black, with a separate driver for quick maintenance and a 3-year warranty.
