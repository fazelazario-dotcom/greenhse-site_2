Why this first
A plan lets us work in real metres instead of guessing. A phone photo of a printed plan is fine — as long as the whole plan is in frame and roughly square-on.

Drop 2 points on something you already know the length of — a door is easiest — then type that length. Everything else scales from there.

What the scale unlocks
Once the app knows metres per pixel it can size fittings to their real cut-out, space them by the rules below, and count what a room actually needs.

This is the whole job. Drag a box around a room and the lights go in by themselves — the right number, in the right places. Do that for every room and you are finished.

Why it matters

You can skip this. Your rooms are lit already. This is only for extras you want on top — strip light under the kitchen benches, star lights in the alfresco ceiling, a light inside a wardrobe.

Nothing you choose here changes a room. To change the light in a room, use Change type of light on that room in step 03.
