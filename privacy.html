Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Greenhse Technologies ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • Contact details — your name, email, phone number and address when you place an order, make an enquiry, request a quote, or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Order information — the products you buy or ask about, and any floor plans or project details you send us for a lighting layout.
  • Website usage — standard analytics such as pages visited and device type. Our layout app stores your plans in your own browser; they are not uploaded unless you choose to send them to us.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders and provide quotes and lighting layouts you request.
  • To answer enquiries and provide product support.
  • To send you our newsletter if you subscribed — every email includes an unsubscribe link.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers who help us run the business — such as delivery couriers and payment processors — and only as needed to serve you.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. Payment details are handled by our payment providers and are not stored on our systems.

Access and correction

You can ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us on (08) 9297 2969 or through our contact page.

Questions or complaints

If you have a privacy question or believe we have mishandled your information, contact us first and we will work to resolve it. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.
